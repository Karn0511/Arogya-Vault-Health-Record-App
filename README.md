## 📖 Overview

**Arogya Vault** is a secure, digital vault for your medical history. It replaces physical files with a unified digital identity.

We use **Generative AI** to help patients understand their health better and give doctors instant access to critical data.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

### 🩺 For Patients

* ✅ **AI Symptom Analysis**: Chat with Gemini to understand your symptoms.
* ✅ **Report Vault**: Upload and secure Reports/X-Rays in the cloud.
* ✅ **Instant Sharing**: Share records via One-Time Password (OTP).

### 👨‍⚕️ For Doctors

* ✅ **Quick Access**: Scan QR/OTP to view patient history instantly.
* ✅ **Smart Dashboard**: Visualize patient trends (BP, Growth) effortlessly.
* ✅ **Privacy First**: Access expires automatically after 1 hour.
